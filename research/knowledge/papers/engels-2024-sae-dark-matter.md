# Decomposing the Dark Matter of SAEs

**Authors:** Engels et al. (2024)  
**Venue:** arXiv / interpretability workshop  
**URL:** _(cited in checkpoint 002)_  
**Status:** complete

## Key claims

- SAE reconstruction error ("dark matter") carries behaviorally relevant information not captured by identified sparse features.
- Interventions on sparse features alone miss compensatory pathways in the error term.
- Explains why SAE interpretability is incomplete even with good feature dictionaries.
