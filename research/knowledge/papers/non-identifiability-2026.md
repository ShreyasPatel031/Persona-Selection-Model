# On the Non-Identifiability of Steering Vectors in LLMs

**Authors:** _(2026 preprint authors)_  
**Venue:** arXiv  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06801  
**Status:** complete

## Key claims

- Infinitely many steering directions can produce behaviorally equivalent outputs.
- Behavioral equivalence does not imply representational uniqueness or interpretability.
- Any sparse decomposition or "the" steering vector claim is underdetermined.
