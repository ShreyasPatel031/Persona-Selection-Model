# SAE Interventions Are Unreliable: Post-Intervention Recovery

**Authors:** Cui et al. (2026)  
**Venue:** arXiv  
**URL:** _(2026 preprint; cited in checkpoint 002)_  
**Status:** complete

## Key claims

- After SAE-based intervention, models can recover suppressed behavior via SAE reconstruction residual.
- ~95.8% behavioral recovery reported in some settings — "dark matter" routes around identified features.
- SAE clamp/ablation effects may be transient or compensatable.
