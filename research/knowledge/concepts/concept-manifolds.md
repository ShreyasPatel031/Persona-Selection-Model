# Concept Manifolds

**Slug:** `concept-manifolds`  
**Level:** foundation  
**Status:** stub

## Definition

Concept manifolds model semantic features as curved low-dimensional surfaces in activation space rather than single linear directions. Single-feature or low-K linear steering may fail when the true geometry is nonlinear.
