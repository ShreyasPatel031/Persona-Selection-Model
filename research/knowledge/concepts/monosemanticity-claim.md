# Monosemanticity Claim

**Slug:** `monosemanticity-claim`  
**Level:** foundation  
**Status:** complete

## Definition

The monosemanticity claim holds that individual SAE features correspond to single interpretable concepts. It is an ideal motivating SAE research; in practice features are often polysemantic and traits require coordinated multi-feature activation.
