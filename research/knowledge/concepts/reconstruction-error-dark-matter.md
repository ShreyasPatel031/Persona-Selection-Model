# Reconstruction Error / Dark Matter

**Slug:** `reconstruction-error-dark-matter`  
**Level:** meta  
**Status:** complete

## Definition

SAE reconstruction error (h − W_dec^T z) is the "dark matter" — variance and behaviorally relevant information not captured by the sparse feature set. Models may route behavior through the error term, limiting interpretability and intervention claims.
