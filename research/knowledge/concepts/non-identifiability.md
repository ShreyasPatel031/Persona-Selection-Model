# Non-Identifiability

**Slug:** `non-identifiability`  
**Level:** meta  
**Status:** complete

## Definition

Non-identifiability means infinitely many steering directions (and sparse decompositions) can produce behaviorally equivalent outputs. You cannot claim "this vector IS the internal representation of trait X" from behavioral steering alone.
